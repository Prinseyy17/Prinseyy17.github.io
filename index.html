About

Prinse Katigbak

Most people know me as Prinseyy. I’m based in Dubai, where I spend a lot of time chasing whatever light is good that day — usually early morning or just before sunset.

Photography came first. Video followed when I wanted still images to move, and coding became another way to turn ideas into something people can actually use. Today that means posters, websites, photo sets and edits — this portfolio collects a selection of each.

Graphic design · Poster series

Fresher’s Party ’26

Duality: Vintage & Futuristic

Five poster concepts for the Fresher’s Party at Bath Spa University’s RAK campus. The theme was duality, so every concept puts an old world and a new one on the same page and lets them meet in a different way — a split grid, a record-sleeve collage, a torn portrait, an engraved dial and a painted cinematic scene.

For
Bath Spa University, RAK Campus

The series

One brief, five directions.

Every concept carries the same core information — event, theme, date and venue — so any one of them could front the campaign. What changes is how the two eras meet.

01—02

Two takes built on a hard split: the page is cut down the middle and each half is given to one era.

01

Split Grid

A cream-and-rust editorial page with a sun dial on the left, a black-and-cyan blueprint with a wireframe globe on the right, joined by a single ampersand.

02

Side A / Side B

A record-sleeve collage: a vinyl disc labelled “Analog Soul” and a chrome form labelled “Future Form”, under heavy condensed type and a synthwave grid.

03—04

Here the two eras overlap instead of sitting side by side — a tear through a portrait, a dial that changes material halfway round.

03

Two Eras

A torn split portrait — a Victorian figure with a pocket watch against a cyber-armoured knight — with blackletter and digital type sharing the footer.

04

Engraved / HUD

An invitation-style layout where one dial is half engraved ornament, half heads-up display. Blackletter on parchment meets cyan line work on black.

05

Painted key art instead of a layout — the most cinematic of the set, and the one carrying the university’s branding.

05

Duality

A gothic city and a planet-filled sky meet behind two figures standing back to back, under a gilded title.

Freelance · Website design

Real Lux

Where care comes first.

Real Lux runs automated massage chairs across the UAE, with more than ten years of placing them in malls, cinemas, amusement parks, hotels, exhibitions and night markets. The site had to do two jobs: sell a ten-minute break to the public, and sell a revenue-share partnership to venue owners. It went from a greyscale wireframe through two visual rounds to a final black-and-gold design.

Client
Real Lux, UAE
Type
Freelance
Scope
Wireframe · UI design · page structure
Built in
Figma

Process

From wireframe to final.

Structure first, then colour, then restraint. Each round kept what worked and cut what didn’t.

  1. 01

    Wireframe

    Greyscale blocks to settle the content order: hero and booking, about, mission and vision, products, locations, contacts.

  2. 02

    First visual pass

    The same structure dressed in black and gold, with the brand mark in colour and a display serif for headings.

  3. 03

    Final direction

    Rebuilt around one promise — Relax. Refresh. Revitalize. — with more space, a tighter grid and gold used sparingly.

Final homepage

Relax. Refresh. Revitalize.

The page moves from feeling to proof: a calm hero, the company story, six smart features, the reasons to choose Real Lux, a 50/50 shared-success model for venue partners, locations, and placement options for any space.

Inside the page

Section by section.

Why Choose Real Lux?
